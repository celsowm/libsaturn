## ■6.7 CMDXA~CMDYD (vertex coordinate data) : Specify coordinates such as clipping area coordinates and sprite drawing vertex coordinates in CMDXA to CMDYD. Up to 4 vertex coordinates (X coordinate, Y coordinate) can be specified.
