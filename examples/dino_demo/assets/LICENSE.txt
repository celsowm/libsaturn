This example redistributes the original GLB and generated Saturn model data
under that license. The original GLB is kept unchanged. The generated C/H
model is a converted derivative: geometry is simplified for the Saturn's
VDP1 budget, textures are baked to indexed color, and animation poses are
sampled and quantized for runtime playback. These changes were made by the
LibSaturn project; pikabobalex did not create or endorse the conversion or
demo.
