# pcm8_stream Third-Party Assets

Both assets are CC0 1.0 and come from OpenGameArt.org; `tools/generate_pcm8_stream_assets.py` downloads them at
build time (or reuses the `audio_showcase` cache). Details are in `../audio_showcase/LICENSES.md`.

- **8 Bit Disco Loop** by cosmac: https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-disco-loop (streamed as signed PCM8, stereo)
- **Explosion** by TinyWorlds: https://opengameart.org/content/explosion-0 (resident PCM16 effect)
